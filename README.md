# EX-02-Cross-Platform-Prompting-Evaluating-Diverse-Techniques-in-AI-Powered-Text-Summarization

## AIM
To evaluate and compare the effectiveness of prompting techniques (zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, role-based) across different AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) in a specific task: text summarization.

## Scenario:
You are part of a content curation team for an educational platform that delivers quick summaries of research papers to undergraduate students. Your task is to summarize a 500-word technical article on "The Basics of Blockchain Technology" using multiple AI platforms and prompting strategies.

Your goal is to determine which combination of prompting technique + platform provides the best summary in terms of:

Accuracy

Coherence

Simplicity

Speed

User experience

## Algorithm
## ALGORITHM

Step 1: Define the Task\
1.1 Select the 500-word article on "The Basics of Blockchain Technology".

1.2 Identify undergraduate students as the target audience.

Step 2: Select AI Platforms

2.1 Choose ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Microsoft Copilot.

2.2 Use the same article on all platforms.

Step 3: Apply Prompting Techniques

3.1 Zero-Shot Prompting – Give direct instructions without examples.

3.2 Few-Shot Prompting – Provide examples before asking for a summary.

3.3 Chain-of-Thought Prompting – Ask the AI to analyse the main concepts systematically.

3.4 Role-Based Prompting – Assign the AI the role of an educational content editor.

Step 4: Generate Summaries

4.1 Enter the prompts into each AI platform.

4.2 Generate summaries using all four techniques.

4.3 Collect and record the generated summaries.

Step 5: Evaluate the Results

5.1 Check accuracy of the summaries.

5.2 Evaluate coherence and logical flow.

5.3 Check simplicity and readability.

5.4 Measure response speed.

5.5 Evaluate user experience.

Step 6: Compare and Analyse

6.1 Prepare a comparison table.

6.2 Assign scores from 1 to 5 for each parameter.

6.3 Calculate the total score.

6.4 Identify the best platform and prompting technique.

Step 7: Conclude the Experiment

7.1 Analyse the experimental results.

7.2 Identify the most effective combination.

7.3 Prepare the final report based on the observations.
